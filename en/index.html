01 · Why

I was done
being a
billboard.

Logo on the chest, logo on the shoes, logo on the cap. And paying for the privilege. At some point I'd had enough of being a walking billboard for brands that tell me who I'm supposed to be.

So: a label with an honest name. Provocatively honest. Fuck Off Fashion catches every eye and flips the bird at all that fake ad crap, with a grin.

If you're going to wear an ad on your chest, make it an honest one.

And the designs look bloody good, too.

03 · Anarchy

Fashion is
for sheep.

Fashion tells you who you are this autumn. It sells you off-the-peg individuality in five sizes and three colours, and everyone ends up in the same jacket.

For us, anarchy means the opposite: maximum individualism. No boss, no trend, no brand, no “everyone's wearing this now”. Just you and your call.

You decide.
Not the label, not the algorithm, not the clique. In the end all that counts is a choice you made yourself and stand behind.
You set the value.
No price tag decides what something's worth. A patched shirt you printed yourself is worth more than any logo.
Nobody leads you.
Not even us. Take our designs, cut them up, make something of your own. Anyone who follows us hasn't got it.
Sharing because we want to.
We give everything away. Not out of duty or morals, but because that's what we decided.

What anarchy isn't, for us: violence or destruction.
If you want to be free, you don't need anyone to push around.

06 · DIY

Four steps.
Zero euros*.

* plus paint. And a bit of nerve.

  1. 01

    Grab a design

    Pick one off the wall and download it in full resolution. No account, no email address, no cookie.

  2. 02

    Buy nothing new

    Use what's already there: the stretched-out shirt, the flea-market jacket, the tote bag at the back of the drawer.

  3. 03

    Print

    Transfer film and a heat press (or just the iron to start with), screen printing at the collective round the corner, or a cardboard stencil and fabric spray paint.

  4. 04

    Pass it on

    Wear it. Give it away. Print ten for your mates. The more it gets copied, the better.
